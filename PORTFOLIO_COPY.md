## 5. Tone Guidance

Use a voice that feels human, calm, thoughtful, and approachable. Avoid overly polished brand language, heavy abstraction, or consultancy jargon. Keep the writing clear, warm, and grounded. The tone should feel like a real person speaking with care and confidence.
